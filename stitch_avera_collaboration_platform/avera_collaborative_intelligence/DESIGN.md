## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a forward-leaning, warm, and hyper-structured aesthetic tailored for modern collaborative intelligence. It balances the pristine clarity of high-performance enterprise tools with an inviting, tactile humanism. 

### Aesthetic Philosophy
The visual language merges **Minimalism** with **Ambient Warm Glow layers** and **Soft Structural Containers**:
- **Clarity over Clutter:** White and cool off-white canvases allow typography and data visualizations to stand out with zero friction.
- **Warm Kinetic Energy:** Energetic citrus and deep orange accents draw immediate attention to key actions, real-time collaboration signals, and high-value workflows without inducing visual fatigue.
- **Physical Softness:** High-curvature cards (roundedness level 2), micro-borders, and diffused, low-opacity ambient shadows provide a soft, layered feel reminiscent of physical cards resting on clean studio desks.

### Target Audience & Tone
Engineers, product thinkers, and cross-functional teams who value clarity, speed, and precision. The emotional signature is calm confidence, collaborative warmth, and unmistakable technical rigor.

## Colors

The palette establishes an energetic yet focused light-mode interface. Surfaces are anchored by soft whites and cool architectural grays, accented by vibrant orange light beams and high-contrast slate typography.

### Surface Tiers
- **Canvas Base (`#F5F6F8` to `#FBFBFC`):** The contextual backing for dashboards, full-screen canvas views, and landing backgrounds.
- **Card Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** Elevated crisp surface for cards, floating toolbars, navigation sidebars, and dialogue inputs.
- **Subtle Fill (`#F1F3F5`):** Secondary container backgrounds, inactive tabs, and search bar tracks.

### Brand & Functional Accents
- **Primary Accent (`#FF6B00`):** Core action color. Applied to primary call-to-actions, active workspace indicators, and critical focus states.
- **Primary Glow (`rgba(255, 107, 0, 0.15)`):** Diffused halo layers used beneath interactive elements, active cards, and ambient landing section separators.
- **Secondary / Ink (`#0F172A`):** Deep charcoal used for authoritative headlines, crisp icon outlines, and secondary pill buttons.
- **Supportive Grays:** Slate (`#64748B`) for body text; Muted Border Slate (`#E2E8F0` and `#E5E7EB`) for 1px hairline card borders.
- **Status Indicators:** Emerald (`#10B981`) for positive velocity, Amber (`#F59E0B`) for work in progress, and Crimson (`#EF4444`) for bottlenecks and alerts.

## Typography

The pairing of **Manrope** for headlines and **DM Sans** for body copy produces a geometric, clean, and highly technical aesthetic.

### Rules of Engagement
- **Tight Headings:** High-level headers (`display-xl`, `headline-lg`) utilize tight tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) and heavy weights (`700`/`800`) to deliver impact in both marketing titles and primary dashboard summaries.
- **Understated Precision:** DM Sans ensures effortless readability across multi-line descriptions, task logs, and tabular data. 
- **Numerical Regularity:** When displaying numerical project metrics or timeline graphs, use tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to ensure vertical alignment across data rows.

## Layout & Spacing

The design system relies on an 8pt base grid with a 12-column fluid structure for responsive web surfaces, alongside a fixed sidebar layout for authenticated software views.

### Structure & Grids
- **Application Shell:** Uses a persistent `260px` left sidebar coupled with a fluid workspace area that accommodates modular widgets, cards, and canvas views.
- **Marketing & Auth Containers:** Centered cards utilizing strict maximum widths (`440px` for auth cards, `1200px` for marketing landing containers).
- **Responsive Adaptations:**
  - **Desktop (≥1024px):** 12 columns, `1.5rem` (24px) gutters, full sidebars visible.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, sidebar collapses to icon navigation rail (`72px`), outer padding drops to `1.25rem`.
  - **Mobile (<768px):** 4 columns, navigation transitions to a bottom bar or slide-out drawer, gutter scales down to `1rem`.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses delicate multi-layer shadows, crisp 1px perimeter outlines, and radiant ambient color washes rather than heavy drop shadows.

### Ambient Glows & Atmosphere
- **Ambient Accent Halo:** Used under focal cards and hero headers with a radial gradient: `radial-gradient(circle, rgba(255, 107, 0, 0.12) 0%, rgba(255, 107, 0, 0) 70%)`.
- **Card Edge Precision:** All elevated surfaces feature a hairline border `1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)` to remain distinct against cool-white backgrounds.

### Shadow Tiers
- **Flat/Zero:** Inside search inputs and secondary panels (`box-shadow: none`).
- **Low (Resting Cards):** `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Mid (Hover & Active Cards):** `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Floating (Popovers, Modals, Primary CTAs):** `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(255, 107, 0, 0.22), 0 1px 3px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.05)`.

## Shapes

The shape profile is set to level `2` (Rounded). This brings an approachable, modern feel without sacrificing enterprise density.

### Radius Implementations
- **Base Components (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Checkboxes, tags, badges, small utility chips.
- **Medium Elements (`0.75rem` - `1rem` / 12px - 16px):** Standard buttons, text input fields, dropdown menus, task list containers.
- **Cards & Paneling (`1rem` - `1.5rem` / 16px - 24px):** Dashboard stat tiles, metrics charts, auth cards, elevated modal dialogs.
- **Full Pill (`9999px`):** Floating navigation pills, primary status badges, active sidebar item pills, and global search trigger fields.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#FF6B00` background, pure white bold text (`label-md`), `12px` border radius or pill-shaped. On hover, apply a `translateY(-1px)` lift paired with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 8px 20px rgba(255, 107, 0, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Soft white background with hairline border `#E2E8F0`, slate text (`#0F172A`). On hover, light wash background `#F8FAFC`.
- **Tertiary Tint:** Pale orange wash (`rgba(255, 107, 0, 0.08)`) with `#FF6B00` text for contextual workspace actions.

### Badges & Chips
- Fully rounded pills (`border-radius: 9999px`) with padding `4px 12px`.
- Pale background tints matching the state: Orange Tint (`rgba(255, 107, 0, 0.1)`), Emerald Tint (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.1)`), Slate Tint (`rgba(100, 116, 139, 0.1)`).
- Typography: `label-sm` with tight kerning.

### Input Fields
- Height of `44px` or `48px` with `12px` curvature or complete pill styling.
- Background pure `#FFFFFF` with `1px solid #E2E8F0`. Placeholder styled in muted slate `#94A3B8`.
- Focus state activates an orange perimeter glow: `border-color: #FF6B00; box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(255, 107, 0, 0.15)`.

### Cards & Metric Tiles
- Pure `#FFFFFF` background with `16px` to `20px` radius.
- Includes a subtle `1px` border (`#F1F3F5`) and low ambient drop shadow.
- Stat tiles feature the metric in `headline-lg` (`Manrope`, 700), paired with an upward directional indicator in emerald green (`#10B981`) and a top-right arrow glyph.

### Navigation & Sidebar
- **Sidebar Background:** Clean `#FBFBFC` with right hairline border.
- **Active Navigation Indicator:** Pill container filled with primary orange (`#FF6B00`) and crisp white typography/icons, or soft orange wash (`rgba(255, 107, 0, 0.1)`) with an orange accent notch.
- **Floating Nav Rails:** Used on public pages as frosted translucent pills (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px); background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`).

### Authentication Views
- Centered standalone card (`440px` width) with `24px` radius resting on the cool off-white background with a top-centered orange ambient glow cone.
- Vertically stacked: Brand logo, concise Manrope heading, pill-form fields, full-width primary CTA, followed by divider and OAuth identity buttons.
